Frequently Asked Questions

Is JCC an official Government or Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)?

No. JCC is strictly an independent, private verification assistance registry owned by Mr. Jahir Narjinary. It does not replace statutory inquiries or clearances issued by Indian state police authorities, passport offices, or government ministries.

What is the processing fee and payment mode?

The processing fee is fixed at ₹100 (INR). Payment is handled directly via UPI QR transfer to our verified office account. No automatic credit card charges or third-party payment gateways are utilized.

How long is an issued JCC clearance certificate valid?

All issued certificates remain valid for exactly six (6) calendar months from the date of formal approval and staff issuance.
